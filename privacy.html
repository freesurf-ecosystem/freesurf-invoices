This Privacy Policy explains how FreeSurf collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our apps, websites, and services. FreeSurf provides free utility tools including invoice generation, link-in-bio pages, social media cross-posting, text-to-speech reading, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Without an account: Most FreeSurf tools work without creating an account. When you use tools without signing in, your data (invoice drafts, generated audio, saved recordings, image generations, transcriptions) is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or have access to this locally stored data.

With an account (optional): If you choose to create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and authentication credentials (via Supabase)
  • Invoice drafts, business profiles, client information, and saved invoices you choose to sync to your account
  • Link-in-bio profile content including display name, bio, links, and avatar images
  • Social media account connections and OAuth tokens for cross-posting (Post tool)
  • Support requests and correspondence

AI Processing: Some FreeSurf tools use AI models to generate results. These models run on our own GPU infrastructure (RunPod). We do not share your content with third-party AI companies such as OpenAI, Google, or Anthropic. Your text, audio, and photos are processed in memory and immediately discarded after the result is returned:

  • Natural Reader: Text is sent to our self-hosted Kokoro TTS server to generate speech audio.
  • Transcriber: Audio is sent to our self-hosted Whisper transcription server.
  • Calorie Tracker: Food photos are sent to our self-hosted vision AI server.
  • Image Maker: Text prompts are sent to image generation services.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our tools and services
  • Authenticate users who choose to create accounts
  • Sync your data across devices when signed in
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send optional product updates or newsletters (only if you opt in)
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your invoice data, link profiles, posts, or AI-generated content for advertising purposes.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Ad Networks

FreeSurf apps display advertisements through third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX, and similar providers. To serve and measure ads, these networks may collect:

  • Identifiers: Device advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)
  • Location: Coarse location derived from IP address (not precise GPS)
  • Usage Data: Product interaction data (e.g., ad views, clicks)
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and performance data

This data is shared with our ad mediation partners solely for ad delivery and measurement. No personal information from your FreeSurf account or tool usage (invoices, transcripts, meal logs, recordings) is shared with advertisers. Ad-supported functionality is disclosed in each app's store listing.

Before personalized ads are served, the app will request permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt or Android's equivalent. You may opt out at any time through your device's privacy settings. Non-personalized ads do not require tracking permission.

4. Third-Party Services

FreeSurf uses the following infrastructure and service providers:

  • Supabase — authentication and account data storage
  • Cloudflare — web hosting, Workers, R2 storage, and KV
  • RunPod — GPU infrastructure for self-hosted AI models (Kokoro TTS, Whisper, vision models)
  • Replicate / Krea / Flux — image generation APIs
  • Google AdMob / AppLovin MAX — in-app advertising

These providers process data on our behalf to deliver specific parts of the service.

5. Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Local data: Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. You can delete your account by contacting us.
  • AI processing: Submitted text, audio, and photos are processed in memory on our servers and returned as output. No user content is retained on AI servers after the result is returned. AI-generated content is saved locally on your device. If you choose to sign in, content may also sync to your account for cross-device access.
  • No model training: User content processed by our AI models is never used to train, fine-tune, or improve the models. Transcripts, audio, and images are used strictly for immediate real-time output.
  • Support requests: May be retained to resolve issues and document outcomes.

6. GDPR & CCPA Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Right to opt out: Opt out of personalized advertising via your device's privacy settings or by limiting ad tracking.
  • Right to data portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@freesurf.tools. We will respond within 30 days. For EEA users, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children's Privacy

FreeSurf is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be noted with an updated effective date.
